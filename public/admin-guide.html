Book Studio Administrator Guide

User management

Open Admin → Users & subscription tiers to create account records, change roles and tiers, disable users or delete users who do not own books. An account record does not create a Cognito identity, password, invitation, or billing subscription. Identity provider provisioning is a separate step.

Role and data isolation

Administrator accounts can manage books and users. Standard accounts should access only their own books. Legacy unowned manuscripts are retained for administrators pending explicit ownership migration. Confirm authorization tests before onboarding customers.

Plans

Basic: $49.99/month, 5 books, 30 chapters/book, 3,000 target words/chapter. Premium: $99.99/month, 25 books, 200 chapters/book, 10,000 target words/chapter. These prices are placeholders for a future billing integration, not invoices.

Operations

Monitor inference availability, chapter reviews and storage. Chapter quality gates and the inference pool are distinct from account and subscription state. Avoid deleting accounts with owned manuscripts until an export, transfer or archive process is available.
